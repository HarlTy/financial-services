### Medicare enrollment rules (stable rules, not annual figures)
- **IEP:** 7 months — the 3 months before the 65th-birthday month, that month, and 3 after.
- **GEP:** Jan 1–Mar 31; coverage starts the first of the month AFTER signup (2023 rule change; older "July 1 start" guidance is obsolete).
- **SEP:** anytime while covered by CURRENT-employment group coverage, plus 8 months after that employment/coverage ends. COBRA and retiree coverage do NOT count and do NOT extend the SEP — the classic trap. Forms CMS-40B + employer CMS-L564.
- **Part B late penalty:** +10% of the premium per full 12-month eligible-but-unenrolled period; permanent.
- **Part D late penalty:** 1% of the national base premium × full uncovered months (63+ days without creditable coverage); permanent.
- **Medigap open enrollment:** one-time 6-month guaranteed-issue window starting the first month the person is both 65+ and enrolled in Part B. Plans C/F closed to those newly eligible after 1/1/2020; G and N are the standard comprehensive choices.
- **HSA interaction:** Medicare enrollment (including retroactive Part A, up to 6 months) ends HSA eligibility — stop HSA contributions roughly 6 months before enrolling when delaying past 65. VERIFY the current retroactivity rule before a client-facing answer.

## 529 plans (SECURE 2.0 §126)

529→Roth IRA rollover: $35,000 lifetime per beneficiary; capped each year by the Roth contribution limit ($7,500 TY2026); the 529 must be open ≥15 years; contributions (and earnings on them) from the last 5 years are ineligible; trustee-to-trustee transfer into the BENEFICIARY's Roth; beneficiary needs earned income ≥ the rollover amount; NO Roth income phase-out applies and NO pro-rata rule. — FINAL

## QBI (§199A) — post-OBBBA

20% deduction is permanent. New $400 minimum deduction where the taxpayer has ≥$1,000 of active QBI. The phase-in RANGE widened to $75,000 single / $150,000 MFJ beginning TY2026. SSTB status (consulting qualifies) phases the benefit out across that range starting at the indexed §199A taxable-income threshold — VERIFY the current-year threshold amount before computing a specific household's deduction. — FINAL structure / VERIFY threshold dollars
