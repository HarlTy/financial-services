"(v) Applicable age.-
(I) In the case of an individual who attains age 72 after December 31, 2022,
and age 73 before January 1, 2033, the applicable age is 73.
(II) In the case of an individual who attains age 74 after December 31, 2032,
the applicable age is 75."
